Biography

Meet Chukwudi Solomon

Business Strategist • Entrepreneur • Educator • Speaker

Chukwudi Solomon is a seasoned business strategist and speaker with a relentless commitment to seeing people and businesses thrive on clear purpose, sound strategy, and unwavering discipline.

Academic & Empirical Foundation

Holding an MSc in Business with Business Analytics from Northumbria University London, Chukwudi brings rigorous analytical modeling, quantitative forecasting, and competitive benchmarking into the realm of everyday entrepreneurship. He does not believe in superficial "growth hacks"; instead, he advocates for deep structural clarity, sustainable unit economics, and long-term value compounding.

The Philosophy of Revealing Purpose

Every sustainable business begins with an authentic "Why". Chukwudi works closely with founders and corporate executives to distill their core calling into scalable operations, customer-centric offerings, and high-performing teams.

"When purpose is clear, strategic decisions become straightforward. When discipline is present, strategic goals become reality."

The Monday Business Snippet Initiative

Recognizing the need for consistent, accessible strategic education, Chukwudi hosts the weekly live Monday Business Snippet series every Monday at 10:00 AM WAT on Google Meet. This open platform serves hundreds of ambitious builders across the continent and diaspora.
