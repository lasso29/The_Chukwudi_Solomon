The Weekly Agenda

What Happens Every Monday at 10:00 AM WAT

Each session is 45 minutes of pure substance, zero filler, followed by direct Q&A.

1. The Strategic Deep-Dive (20 Mins)

Chukwudi breaks down a single core business concept (e.g. pricing power, customer acquisition channels, cash-flow safeguards).

2. Case Analysis & Analytics (15 Mins)

Real-world teardown of an African or global business model, examining empirical metrics and why it succeeded or failed.

3. Live Builder Hot-Seat (10 Mins)

Open interactive floor where participants can pose current operational or marketing dilemmas for direct real-time feedback.
